Shoe Store AI Publisher — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 17, 2026

This policy describes the intended operation of Shoe Store AI Publisher. Update it to accurately reflect your deployed application before publishing.

Information processed

When TikTok access is enabled, the application may process OAuth authorization tokens, authorized account identifiers, creator posting settings, upload status, and the video content and captions selected for publishing. It also processes local product images and licensed audio chosen by the operator.

Purpose and sharing

Information is used to create videos, manage account authorization, upload approved content and check publishing results. Information necessary to publish is sent to TikTok through its official API. Locally configured AI caption generation may use Ollama. The application does not intentionally sell account data.

Storage and security

In the local deployment, generated media and logs are stored on the operator's computer. If OAuth is enabled, tokens are stored locally with restricted file permissions. No security measure guarantees absolute protection.

Retention and deletion

The operator may delete local generated files, logs and stored OAuth tokens. Access can also be revoked through TikTok account settings. Contact the app operator to request deletion of information under their control.
